On **October 10, 2026**, the Unipol Forum in Assago hosts **Kid Yugi** with his **"Anche gli Eroi Muoiono Tour"** ("Even Heroes Die Tour"), an arena date that brings one of the most followed names in the new Italian rap scene to Milan. For anyone travelling from outside the region, booking accommodation ahead of time avoids nasty surprises, especially on a date that draws a large, young crowd from across Lombardy.

## Why stay in Piacenza instead of Assago

Piacenza is comfortably connected to Milan by train, a practical alternative to booking directly in the Milan hinterland, where availability runs out fast on concert nights and prices climb. Choosing Piacenza as your base, you can reach Assago via the rail network and local transport, then head back in the evening to a quieter city, away from the traffic of the urban belt.

## A private patio to unwind after the concert

Roma284 is located in **Piacenza's historic centre**, just **900 metres (about a 9-minute walk) from the train station**. After an evening of loud music, the apartment's real added value is the **private patio**: an outdoor space all to yourself where you can sit back, get some air, and wind down the day without the noise of a shared hotel or hostel. It's a detail that makes all the difference for anyone looking for a short but genuinely restorative stay after a live event.

The apartment also offers self check-in with a smart lock, handy if you're getting back late from the arena, an equipped kitchen, 1 Gbit/s fibre WiFi, and always welcomes dogs and cats free of charge.
