**Mercanteinfiera Spring** returns to **Fiere di Parma** from **Saturday 27 February to Sunday 7 March 2027**: **9 days** of international fair, with time to explore it at a relaxed pace, even over several visits. If you are coming from out of town, or working in the area and want a comfortable place to stay, basing yourself in Piacenza is a practical choice.

## The event at a glance

Mercanteinfiera is an international-level event held in Parma at **Fiere di Parma**. The official calendar lists the dates as **27 February to 7 March 2027**. For opening hours, tickets and entry details, check the [official event website](https://www.aefi.it/it/manifestazione/mercanteinfiera-2027).

If you are interested in other dates in the area, you will find the up-to-date calendar on the page about [fairs and events in Emilia](/en/trade-fairs/).

## Why stay in Piacenza

Piacenza is a quiet city with a compact historic centre that is easy to explore on foot. Roma284 is at **Via Roma 284**, about **900 m (~9 minutes on foot)** from the station: handy if you arrive by train and want to avoid traffic and parking.

To get to Parma, the railway line and the A1 motorway let you plan your day independently. Always check up-to-date timetables before you leave; the guide on [how to get here](/en/getting-here/) has useful directions. Piacenza is also conveniently linked to Milan by train, which helps if your trip includes several stops.

## The fully equipped kitchen: an advantage on fair days

If you spend several days among halls and stands, a simple meal at home at the end of the day makes a difference. The apartment has a **fully equipped kitchen**: you can have a relaxed breakfast before heading out, cook a light dinner when you return, or store what you pick up from local markets and shops. It also suits longer stays, and travellers as a couple or with family (up to **4 guests**, **50 m²**, 2 rooms).

Plus: **1 Gbit/s fibre WiFi** for working in the evening, a **private patio** to unwind, and **self check-in with smart lock from 3:00 PM** (check-out by 10:00 AM), useful if your schedule changes. **Pets always welcome, free of charge.**
