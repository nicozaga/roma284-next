If you have a trade fair in Parma and the hotels in town are fully booked, or priced in a way that doesn't work for you, you're not alone. Cibus, Cibus Tec and Mercanteinfiera attract a professional crowd that books early, and anyone deciding at the last minute is often left with no choice. One practical solution is to stay in Piacenza, on the same route.

## Piacenza and Parma: the journey in brief

Piacenza and Parma sit on the same railway line and the same motorway, the A1, and are also linked by the Via Emilia. The connection is direct and straightforward, with no complicated changes. By car you follow the A1 or the Via Emilia; by train you reach Parma easily, with frequent services throughout the day. Exact timetables vary, so check the operator's website the day before.

One detail not to forget: Parma railway station is not the fairground. To reach the halls you need a final leg by shuttle, taxi or car, following the instructions the organiser publishes for each event. Allow extra time, especially on peak days.

If you are flying in, the usual approach is to land at Milan and continue by train: Piacenza is conveniently connected to Milan by rail, so it works well as a base on the way to Parma, whichever airport you come in through.

## Who it makes sense for

Staying in Piacenza is a good choice if:

- **You are an exhibitor or a buyer** and need a place to really rest, with a fully equipped kitchen for a relaxed breakfast or a light dinner after long days on your feet.
- **You travel as a group or with a colleague**: the apartment has two rooms and sleeps up to 4, handy for sharing the cost of a stay of several nights.
- **You come from Milan or north-west Italy, or are visiting several cities**: Piacenza is conveniently connected to Milan by train, so it makes a sensible stopover.
- **You bring your dog**: pets are always welcome, free of charge.
- **You need to work in the evening**: the 1 Gbit/s fibre WiFi handles calls and large file transfers without trouble, and there is a private patio to unwind for a moment.

Roma284 is at Via Roma 284, in the historic centre, and it is quiet in the evening. The station is about 900 m away, roughly a ten-minute walk. Self check-in with a smart lock is available from 3:00 pm, useful if you arrive late after a day at the fair; check-out is at 10:00 am.

## Who it's less convenient for

Let's be clear: staying in Piacenza adds a transfer. If you want to be within walking distance of the halls, if you like dining with clients near the fairground until late, or if you have a dawn entry slot, staying in Parma remains more practical, provided you can find a room. Anyone who doesn't drive and has very tight morning schedules should also factor in the train and the final leg to the fair.

The compromise works best for stays of two or more nights, where the quality of your rest and a proper space count for more than the minutes saved. For dates and the events calendar, see the page on [trade fairs near Piacenza](/en/trade-fairs/).

## In short

- Piacenza is on the same route as Parma (train, A1, Via Emilia): an alternative when hotels are full.
- It suits stays of several nights, groups of colleagues, and anyone who values a kitchen, a patio and fast WiFi.
- Dogs always welcome, free of charge; self check-in from 3:00 pm.
- Not ideal if you want to be a short walk from the halls or have tight dawn schedules.
- Check the event's timetables and shuttles on the official channels; by booking direct you can save up to 15% less.
