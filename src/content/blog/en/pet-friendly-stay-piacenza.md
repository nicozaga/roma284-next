If you travel with a dog, you know the drill: half the search isn't about the place, it's about whether they really accept your pet. Between "pets allowed on request", size limits and surcharges that appear at the very end, you can lose a lot of time. This guide explains what to expect when staying in Piacenza with a dog, and who it suits best.

## Piacenza with a dog: the practical answer

Piacenza is a compact city, which is an advantage with a dog: the historic centre is easy to explore on foot, with no need to take the car for every outing. The station is about 900 m away (roughly a ten-minute walk), so you can reach the centre without any transport. That also makes arriving by train with your dog simple, for example from Milan, which is [conveniently connected by train](.) with Piacenza. To see why it can make sense to stay here rather than in the big city, read our guide on [where to stay near Milan](/en/stay-near-milan/).

## Walks and breaks: what changes in the historic centre

Staying in the centre means you step outside and you're already walking: early morning, the last evening stroll, a midday break if you work remotely. You don't need to find parking or drive anywhere for your dog's needs. The centre has a quiet rhythm, especially in the evening, which matters if your dog is sensitive to noise.

One tip that applies everywhere: bring water and a bowl for your outings, and check the rules of individual venues beforehand, because access to restaurants depends on each owner and we never take it for granted.

## Roma284: patio, kitchen and clear rules

Roma284 is a 50 m² apartment at Via Roma 284, in the historic centre: two rooms, up to 4 guests. Pets are always welcome and free of charge: no surcharge, no negotiation. Two things make a real difference with a dog:

- the private patio, handy for a quick break or some fresh air without going out every time;
- the fully equipped kitchen, useful if you have kibble, bowls and a feeding schedule to keep.

Self check-in with a smart lock is from 3:00 pm, on your own: no waiting at a reception desk with your dog on the lead. Check-out is at 10:00 am. The 1 Gbit/s fibre WiFi helps if you work remotely.

### The limits, honestly

The patio is not a garden: if you have a very large, very active dog, it will still need green spaces to run, reached with a walk. The historic centre is also an urban setting: if your dog doesn't like pavements, bikes and crowds, choose the quieter hours. Finally, with a maximum of 4 guests and two rooms, space may be tight for large groups with several dogs.
